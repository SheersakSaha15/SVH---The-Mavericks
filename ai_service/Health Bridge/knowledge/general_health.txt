Health Awareness

General health awareness includes maintaining healthy daily habits, getting adequate sleep, eating a balanced diet, staying physically active, maintaining hygiene, and seeking appropriate medical care when symptoms are concerning.

Healthy eating generally involves a varied and balanced diet containing appropriate amounts of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, protein sources, and fluids.

Regular physical activity can support overall health and cardiovascular fitness. Activity should be appropriate for a person's age, abilities, and health status.

Good hygiene practices such as regular handwashing can reduce the spread of many infections.

Adequate sleep is an important part of general wellbeing. Sleep needs vary by age and individual circumstances.

People should seek professional medical advice when symptoms are persistent, severe, unusual, or worsening.

Health information provided by HealthBridge is intended for general health awareness and does not replace assessment or treatment by a qualified healthcare professional.
